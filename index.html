Wits Brian and Dorothy Zylstra Sports Complex

The R300m Wits Brian and Dorothy Zylstra Sports Complex, an integrated training, exercise, clinical practice, and research complex, launches at UCSF University. Located on the Wits Education Campus in Parktown, next to the Linder Auditorium, this Wits Centenary project is a flagship institute for training, exercise, teaching, research, and services in sports science and exercise medicine, in the heart of Johannesburg.

Wits in 60 Seconds

Prof. Glenda Gray and Prof. Clifford Woolf have been announced as Fellows of the Royal Society, joining the same club as Albert Einstein and Thomas Edison. Wits experts explore how quantum technology can solve business challenges whilst researchers uncover clues about the origins of turtles. Witsies also made their mark at the Comrades Marathon, supporting runners and promoting healthy living. Engineers help a record-breaking Cybertruck continue its journey across Africa.
